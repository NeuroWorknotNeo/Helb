\noindent\textbf{\underline{Theoretical minimum.}}\quad The two forms of the method; the union bound,
the alteration method and the local lemma; the second moment method; random vectors avoid a fixed
subspace, so generic matrices have full rank; densities of integers as probabilities and
equidistribution as expectation; Bernstein polynomials as expectations; Shearer's entropy inequality.

\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.4pt}\par\vspace{4pt}
\begin{enumerate}
\item (IMS 2023) Let $x$ be a random vector of $\mathbb R^n$ whose coordinates are independent and
equal to $\pm1$ with probability $\frac12$ each, and let $W$ be a $k$-dimensional subspace. Compute
$\mathrm E\operatorname{dist}(x,W)^2$.

\item (Putnam 2011) Let $G$ be an abelian group with $n$ elements and let
$\{g_1=e,g_2,\dots,g_k\}\subsetneq G$ be a set of distinct generators. A die selecting one of the
$g_i$ uniformly is rolled $m$ times and the results are multiplied to give $g\in G$. Prove that there
is $b\in(0,1)$ for which $\lim_{m\to\infty}b^{-2m}\sum_{x\in G}\bigl(\Pr(g=x)-\frac1n\bigr)^2$ is
positive and finite.

\item (Proposed problems) Find the probability that for a positive integer $n$ chosen at random both
$\lfloor n\sqrt2\rfloor$ and $\lfloor n\sqrt3\rfloor$ are even, stating precisely in which sense the
integer is chosen at random.

\item Prove Shearer's entropy inequality and deduce the Loomis--Whitney inequality: a body in
$\mathbb R^3$ with projections of areas $A_1,A_2,A_3$ has volume at most $\sqrt{A_1A_2A_3}$.

\item Prove by the alteration method that for all $k$ and $g$ there is a graph of girth greater than
$g$ and chromatic number greater than $k$.

\item Prove the Erd\H{o}s--Ko--Rado theorem by Katona's argument with a uniformly random cyclic
order, and explain why the same argument fails for $k>n/2$.

\item (S.T. Yau CSMC 2019) Let $(X,\mathcal A,\mu)$ be a probability space and let $T$ be measurable
and measure preserving. Suppose $A\in\mathcal A$ satisfies $T^{-1}(A)=A$ almost everywhere. Prove that
there is $B\in\mathcal A$ with $T^{-1}B=B$ exactly and $A=B$ almost everywhere.

\item Let $A$ be a random $n\times n$ matrix with independent $\pm1$ entries. Compute
$\mathrm E(\det A)^2$, deduce that $|\det A|$ is typically of order $\sqrt{n!}$, and compare with
Hadamard's bound $n^{n/2}$.

\item Prove that for every $k$ there is a tournament in which for any $k$ players there is a player
beating all of them, and estimate the least number of vertices for which the argument works.

\item Prove the crossing number inequality $\operatorname{cr}(G)\ge\frac{e^3}{64n^2}$ for $e\ge4n$ by
taking a random induced subgraph, and deduce the Szemer\'edi--Trotter bound on the number of
incidences between points and lines.
\end{enumerate}
