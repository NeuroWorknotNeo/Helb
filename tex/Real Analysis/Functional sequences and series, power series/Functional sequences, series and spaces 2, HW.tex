\noindent\textbf{\underline{Theoretical minimum.}}\quad Weierstrass' test gives uniform and absolute convergence simultaneously
and therefore never applies to a conditionally convergent series; there one uses the uniform
versions of the Dirichlet and Abel tests, which are Abel summation with the estimates taken
uniformly in $x$. A uniformly convergent series of continuous functions has a continuous sum and
can be integrated term by term; to differentiate term by term one needs uniform convergence of the
differentiated series, not of the original one. To build a function with prescribed bad points, put
a scaled copy of a singularity at each of them: $\sum2^{-n}\varphi(x-r_n)$ and $\sum\{nx\}/n^2$ are
the two standard machines. Finally, a series whose terms have a fixed sign behaves like a numerical
series with a parameter, and for it uniform convergence follows from continuity of the sum on a
compact set --- Dini's theorem in the form for series.

\noindent\rule{\textwidth}{0.4pt}\par\medskip

\begin{enumerate}[itemsep=0.6em]
\item Let $(r_n)$ be an enumeration of the rationals in $[0,1]$. Prove that
$F(x)=\sum_{n\ge1}2^{-n}|x-r_n|$ is continuous on $[0,1]$, that $F$ is not differentiable at any
rational, and that $F$ is differentiable at almost every point.

\item Prove that $\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{(-1)^n}{n+x}$ converges uniformly on $[0,\infty)$ but not
absolutely at any point, and find $\lim_{x\to\infty}$ of the sum together with the first term of
its asymptotics.

\item (ISTCiM 2021) Prove that $\sum\limits_{n,m=1}^N\frac{(-1)^{n+m}}{n^nm^m(n+m)^2}>0$ for every
positive integer $N$.

\item Prove that $W(x)=\sum_{n\ge0}a^n\cos(b^nx)$ with $0<a<1$, $ab>1$ is continuous on $\mathbb R$
and that the series may not be differentiated term by term; state the condition under which $W$ is
nowhere differentiable and say where this is proved (Differentiable functions -- 8).

\item Prove that $\zeta(x)=\sum_{n\ge1}n^{-x}$ is infinitely differentiable on $(1,\infty)$, that the
differentiated series converges uniformly on $[1+\delta,\infty)$ but not on $(1,\infty)$, and that
$\zeta(x)\sim\frac1{x-1}$ as $x\to1^+$.

\item Find the set of $x$ for which $\sum_{n\ge1}\frac{x^n}{1+x^n}$ converges, prove that the
convergence is uniform on compact subsets of $(-1,1)$, and find the asymptotics of the sum as
$x\to1^-$.

\item Prove Dini's theorem for series: if $u_n\ge0$ are continuous on a compact $K$ and $\sum u_n$
has a continuous sum, then the convergence is uniform. Give a counterexample when the terms are
allowed to change sign.

\item Give an example of a series of $C^\infty$ functions which converges uniformly, whose
differentiated series converges pointwise everywhere, and for which the sum of the differentiated
series is nevertheless not the derivative of the sum.

\item Prove that if $\sum u_n$ converges uniformly on a set $E$ and all $u_n$ are continuous, then it
converges uniformly on the closure of $E$. Deduce that for continuous terms uniform convergence on
the rationals of $[0,1]$ implies uniform convergence on $[0,1]$.

\item Let $u_n$ be continuous on $[0,1]$ with $\sum u_n$ converging pointwise to a continuous sum.
Prove that for every $\varepsilon>0$ every subinterval of $[0,1]$ contains an interval on which the
tails are eventually smaller than $\varepsilon$ uniformly, and show by an example that the
convergence need not be uniform on any subinterval. (Use the Baire category theorem, Continuous
functions -- 8.)
\end{enumerate}
